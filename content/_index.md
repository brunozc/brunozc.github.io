---

I study how soils respond to dynamic and cyclic loading, and what that means for the infrastructure built on them.
Most of my work is on railways: the propagation of train-induced waves through the ground, the critical speed of
trains on soft soils and the long-term settlement of the railway track. I combine numerical modelling with field data and
machine learning, and I release the resulting models as open-source software for engineers and researchers.
